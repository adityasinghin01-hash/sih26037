## Why — and the project already knew

`plan/CLAIM-LEDGER.md`, its own scope note at lines 119–129:

> *its geometry is asserted and real (free width **3.830 m**, margin **0.965 m each side**,
> cow motionless to 1 µm over 45.2 s).*
>
> *So "we pass the cow with 0.95 m clearance" is **forbidden as a planner claim** and **true
> as a statement about the scenario geometry**. Say which one you mean, every time.*

**0.965 m is geometry, not a result.** It is (3.830 − 1.900) ÷ 2 — half the spare width of
the gap the scenario hands the car. It is not something the planner achieved.

**M6 is the real thing.** `writeDemoResults.m` line 140: *"minimum separation to any of the
three scripted actors, world frame, rectangle-projection method."* That is what the planner
actually did, and today it is **0.618 m**.

The claim ledger contradicts itself: Part 1 quotes 0.965 m as a planner claim backed by
`demo_play('demo1')`, while its own scope note two hundred lines down forbids exactly that.

## What is still genuinely true — do not lose this

- The planner **completes the full 610 m route**, **0 plan failures**. Verified today.
- It **negotiates past a cow that never moves** — the probe fires (`PROBE` at t = 35 s,
  v = 0.08 m/s), then it commits and drives on. That is the whole mechanism working.
- **0 imminent barrier violations.** The raw count of 387 is the documented false-positive
  (any stationary object anywhere ahead), which is why the imminent counter exists.
