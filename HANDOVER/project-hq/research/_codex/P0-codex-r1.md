## P0 — SIH26037 rules and sources

**1. Problem statement.** The [official SIH26037 listing](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS) gives the title **“Adaptive Path Planning and Collision Avoidance for Autonomous Vehicles on Unstructured Indian Roads.”** Organization and department: **MathWorks**; category: **Software**; theme: **Smart Vehicles**. The full wording is on that page; the following is a close paraphrase of its requirements.

Build a MATLAB/Simulink simulation that combines camera/LiDAR/radar perception, short-term prediction of irregular road-user movement, decision logic, real-time collision-free replanning, and vehicle motion. Test at least five situations: an unmarked village road, an unsignalized urban intersection, a slow-vehicle highway merge, a dense mixed-traffic market, and a sudden cattle crossing. Include at least two detailed RoadRunner scenes. The stated **finished-solution deliverables** are the simulation model, scenes, results for replanning latency/path smoothness/scenario completion rate, a short technical report, and a demo video. The listing does **not** say all those assets must accompany the idea PPT. [Official listing](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS)

Permitted data includes Automated Driving Toolbox datasets, RoadRunner samples, team-created scenarios, and public Indian traffic data. The listing names the live [India Driving Dataset](https://idd.insaan.iiit.ac.in/). Its other named source, “Mendeley traffic dataset,” has a truncated URL (`https://data.mend`) on the [SIH listing](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS); the intended dataset is **UNVERIFIED**.

**2. Timeline and submitter.** The live [SIH26037 row](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS) states **30 September 2026** for idea submission. A [university-hosted copy of SIH 2026 guidelines](https://sih-uit.vercel.app/assets/sih-2026-guidelines.pdf) instead says **15 September** and contains a stale “SIH2025” label. These sources conflict; use the live PS deadline while the SPOC checks what the team’s portal actually permits.

Under that [2026 guidelines copy](https://sih-uit.vercel.app/assets/sih-2026-guidelines.pdf), the **college SPOC nominates** internal-round teams; the **nominated team leader** then logs in, enters the idea, and uploads its PDF. Experts evaluate submissions; selections appear on the portal and are emailed. About **4–5 teams per PS may** reach the finale. The copy proposes an **offline finale at nodal centres in December 2026**. An exact shortlist announcement date and exact finale dates are **UNVERIFIED**. The SPOC/leader sequence also appears in [SIH-hosted 2024 guidance](https://www.sih.gov.in/letters/Guidelines-College-SPOC.pdf).

**3. Idea PPT.** [SIH’s 2026 PPTX template](https://sih.gov.in/letters/2026/SIH2026-IDEA-Presentation-Format.pptx) is linked by [MGIT’s SIH 2026 page](https://mgit.ac.in/events/mgit-internal-hackathon-smart-india-hackathon-sih-2026/). An [accessible copy of the template text](https://www.scribd.com/document/1075380264/SIH2026-IDEA-Presentation-Format) specifies **at most six submitted slides, title included**: title; idea/proposed solution; technical approach; feasibility/viability; impact/benefits; research/references. Delete the seventh instruction slide, keep the prescribed headings, favour points and diagrams, and **upload PDF**, not PPTX. A national portal **file-size limit is UNVERIFIED**. [Template text](https://www.scribd.com/document/1075380264/SIH2026-IDEA-Presentation-Format)

**4. National screening.** The [2026 guidelines copy, p. 19](https://sih-uit.vercel.app/assets/sih-2026-guidelines.pdf) lists novelty, complexity, clarity and detail in the prescribed format, feasibility, practicability, sustainability, scale of impact, user experience, and potential for future progression. The same list appears in [SIH-hosted 2024 guidance](https://www.sih.gov.in/letters/Guidelines-College-SPOC.pdf). **2026 criterion weights and separate MathWorks evaluator guidance are UNVERIFIED.** SIH26037’s own [required scenarios and performance evidence](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS) are the clearest PS-specific requirements to address.

**5. Automated screening and a practice scorer.** I found **no official evidence** that SIH uses AI, keyword matching, or automated scoring to shortlist idea PPTs. The available [guidelines describe expert evaluation](https://sih-uit.vercel.app/assets/sih-2026-guidelines.pdf); that does not prove automation is absent. For a **practice review only**, [ChatGPT Free supports file uploads](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275245-using-chatgpt-s-free-tier-faq), including [PDF and PPTX](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983675-what-types-of-files-are-supported). Upload the PS text, template and draft PDF, then ask it to score each published criterion **without invented weights**, cite the slide evidence for each score, and flag every missing PS deliverable. Its score would **not** predict SIH’s decision.

**6. MathWorks precedent.** MathWorks’ [winners archive](https://www.mathworks.com/academia/students/competitions/hackathons/winners.html) names **Team TwinX** as its SIH 2025 PS winner. Its [Student Lounge account](https://blogs.mathworks.com/student-lounge/2026/04/06/from-real-roads-to-real-simulations-how-team-twinx-won-smart-india-hackathon-2025/) describes turning real road data into simulation-ready traffic scenarios with Indian traffic behaviour and hazards. The archive names **Team Solar Masters** as its 2024 winner; its [account](https://blogs.mathworks.com/student-lounge/2025/06/13/innovation-meets-excellence-solar-masters-winning-journey-at-smart-india-hackathon-2024/) describes a MATLAB/Simulink/Simscape single-axis solar tracker with hardware and monitoring. These are examples of completed work, **not published judging weights**. MathWorks’ [hackathon page](https://www.mathworks.com/academia/students/competitions/hackathons.html) describes originality and technical knowledge among past winners; the [SIH26037 listing](https://www.sih.gov.in/sih2026PS) specifically asks for realistic scenarios and closed-loop performance evidence.

**Could not verify:** the intended Mendeley dataset/link; a 2026 national PPT size limit; exact shortlist and finale dates; screening weights; separate MathWorks SIH26037 evaluator instructions; or SIH use of automated PPT screening.
