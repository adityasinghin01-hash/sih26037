### How to use in the sim — JUDGMENT

1. **Pedestrian yielding:** Keep yield propensity site-dependent; do not set a national or vehicle-class prior from the two incomplete studies.
2. **Critical gaps:** Use Mohan–Chandra Table 10 as a conditional junction-model baseline, then vary conflicting large-vehicle share; keep roundabout parameters separate.
3. **2W lateral movement:** Calibrate passing clearance against the speed-dependent pair curves; retain the seepage result as a separate manoeuvre.
4. **Cattle:** Use Raipur’s route density only for a Raipur-like stress scenario; derive encounter rates from your own road observation.
5. **Wrong-way:** Use the Hyderabad count as a site stress case; crash shares cannot set the fraction of actors spawned wrong-way.
6. **Speeds:** Use the 2022 Bengaluru distributions as road-class scenario checks, and the Hyderabad values for a slow commercial-road scenario.
