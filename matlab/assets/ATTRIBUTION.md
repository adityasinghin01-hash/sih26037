## What was deliberately NOT used
A 680-face **"Low-poly cow - neutral pose"** was the best size match and was **rejected**: it is
**CC Attribution-ShareAlike**, and ShareAlike is viral — it would force the whole derivative work,
including our own code and scenarios, under CC BY-SA. Not acceptable for a competition entry.
Models under Sketchfab's **"Free Standard"** licence were also avoided: it restricts
redistribution of the model itself, which is murkier than CC BY for a submission.

**Rule for anything added later: CC BY or public domain only. Never ShareAlike, never
NonCommercial.** NonCommercial would also be a problem — a hackathon entry that leads to a
product is not clearly non-commercial.
