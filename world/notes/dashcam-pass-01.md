## Three things that decide whether a render reads as India
1. **HAZE.** Daylight distance fades to near-white. Very strong atmospheric perspective.
   Clear air is the single biggest tell of a synthetic image.
2. **GAPS OF ABOUT A METRE.** Not a stylistic choice - measured off the frames. Any simulation
   with a comfortable safety envelope will look wrong.
3. **HEADLIGHT BLOOM at night.** Huge white flare, not tidy point lights.

# Pass 02 — close reading, 2 Sep

## The road itself (drive_02, drive_03 — rural)
- Width **~5-6 m total**, single carriageway.
- **Solid white edge line on BOTH sides. NO centre line.** This is the pattern on
  every rural frame. Lines set in ~30-40 cm from the true edge, painted, slightly worn.
- Surface is **mid-grey, dusty, not black**. Smooth, in reasonable condition.
- The road sits slightly **proud** of the land - shoulders fall away.

## The verges are ASYMMETRIC and that matters
Never the same on both sides in any frame:
- one side **tall dense scrub / sugarcane, 1.5-2 m**, right up to the tarmac, overhanging
- the other side **short grass or bare pale-tan dirt**, wide, dusty
Symmetrical verges are an instant tell that a scene is synthetic.

## Power poles - the signature vertical
**Two uprights with X-bracing between them**, cross-arms and insulators on top.
Not a single pole. Appears in nearly every rural frame, at regular spacing, always
on one side only. Wires sag in multiple parallel runs across the frame diagonally.

## Town road (drive_07)
Worn dusty tarmac, **no visible markings at all**, ragged edges. Roadside stalls
standing directly on the dirt shoulder under trees. Rail overbridge with a painted
red banner. Registration **UP13** - western Uttar Pradesh, the team's own region.

## HAZE - the single most important thing
In every daylight frame the horizon fades to **pale pink-white and disappears**.
In drive_02 it is full morning fog: trees 100 m away are barely visible.
This is not subtle atmosphere - it is the dominant feature of the image.
**A clear render will never look like these frames.**

## Light
Dawn / dusk in most clips: pink-orange low sky, long soft light, no hard shadows.
Night clips: huge white headlight bloom, everything else near black.
