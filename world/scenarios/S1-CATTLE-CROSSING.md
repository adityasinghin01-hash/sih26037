# S1 · THE CATTLE CROSSING
**Location: the real map, centre (−280, +450), radius 205 m.** North of the town, where the
built-up edge gives out into fields. Inherits everything in [S0].

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY THERE  (measured off the map, not invented)
783 m of road in six pieces, and **only one junction**:
- a **tertiary road, 209 m, running 133°** — the through road, our ego's road
- a **residential lane, 221 m at 66°**, and another 111 m at 44° — village lanes joining it
- two **living_streets** (157 m at 226°, 56 m at 316°) — genuinely narrow, 3.2 m
- an unclassified stub, 29 m
- **the junction at (−252.8, +373.3), where three roads meet** — no signal, no markings
This is the edge of settlement: a district road with lanes feeding it, houses thinning out,
fields taking over. That is exactly the right place for cattle to be on the road.

## THE ROAD
Tertiary, **7.0 m carriageway**, centre line 3 m mark + 6 m gap at 100 mm (open country),
**edge lines 150 mm set 150 mm in**, both worn to about 60 %.
Surface mid-grey dusty bitumen, aggregate showing in the wheel paths. Camber 2.5 %.
Edges crumble into dirt over a ragged 100–300 mm band. **No kerb.** Earthen shoulders 1.2 m.
**9 potholes** clustered, not spaced: at 34, 88, 89, 141, 196, 240, 241, 243, 302 m along the
through road. Diameters 0.25–0.9 m, 30–90 mm deep. Three patched with darker fresh mix.
**Speed breaker at 268 m**, IRC 99: 3.7 m long, 100 mm high, 17 m radius, parabolic, painted
in black-and-white bands 300 mm wide, badly worn, **no advance sign** — the Indian reality.
**A hume pipe culvert at 158 m**, 900 mm, half silted, splayed wing walls, water stain below.

## ANIMALS
**THE COW — the subject.** Zebu. Withers 128 cm, height with hump 146 cm, body 205 cm long,
64 cm wide, hump 15 cm, horns 28 cm. Light fawn, paler underside, darker muzzle — matched to
the animal on the verge in **drive_08_t22**.
Starts on the **left verge at 296 m**, 1.4 m off the carriageway, standing in a trampled dish
2 m across and 90 mm deep, head down, facing the road at 70°.
Walk 1.2 m/s, stride 1.68 m, **43 strides a minute** — match or the hooves slide.
Other cattle: 3 tethered at the dairy yard (2 standing, 1 lying) + 1 calf. **2 zebu bulls**
loose in the stubble field, horns 40–46 cm, heavy humps. **4 buffalo** (Murrah, 133–142 cm,
curled horns, jet black) being driven along the right verge at 320 m by one of the 21 people.
Dogs: 5 — 2 asleep at the shop, 2 trotting the verge, 1 nursing under the charpai.
Goats: 6, browsing the kans grass at 150–200 m, one up on its hind legs at a branch.
Birds: ~45. 12 crows (5 on the wires at 0.4–1.2 m spacing, never even), 8 mynas on the ground
at the dairy, 10 pigeons wheeling over the village, **8 egrets with the cattle**, 2 kites
circling at 80 m, 5 bulbuls in the grove.
**Different tolerance is the point:** crows and mynas flush at ~8 m, pigeons all at once, the
kites and egrets ignore the car completely.
Insects: ~180 motes, only in the two volumes the low sun backlights — through the mango grove
and along the kans grass edge. **Not everywhere. Everywhere is the mistake.**

## THE ACTION — 62 seconds
t=0     Ego at chainage 0, **52 km/h**, open road, sun low on the right, haze.
t=6.4   Motorcycle overtakes on the right, 0.9 m lateral gap.
t=11.2  **Oncoming motorcycle on OUR side of the road**, closing. Ego moves left onto the
        shoulder and slows to 34. The rider drifts back across at the last moment.
t=19.8  Tractor-trolley oncoming, cane overhanging. Ego edges left again.
t=26.1  Speed breaker at 268 m. Down to 22 km/h, over, accelerate.
t=29.0  **The cow becomes visible at 42 m** — the kans grass and the fodder stack hid it, and
        the road crests slightly here. Geometry decides this, not a script.
t=30.7  Cow steps onto the carriageway. Ego has 26 m at 38 km/h ≈ 2.5 s.
        **BASELINE: emergency stop — and it never moves again.**
t=32.8  Cow reaches the centreline (2.6 m at 1.2 m/s) and **STOPS. Stands. Head turns away.**
        It is not negotiating. It does not care that we are there.
t=32.8  **OURS: probe.** Creep at 0.5 m/s, offset right, read the response.
t=35.2  No response — classified non-negotiable. Planner **measures** the gap: carriageway
        7.0 m, cow occupies 2.5–3.2 m from the left edge, free width right = 3.8 m, ego 1.7 m
        (1.9 m with mirrors), margin 0.95 m each side. **It fits.**
t=36.6  **Auto-rickshaw oncoming at 55 m.** The right-hand gap is closing. Ego **ABORTS** and
        holds. Two futures, commit only to what is safe under both.
t=41.9  Auto passes. Gap reopens.
t=42.7  Ego commits. Crawls past at 8 km/h with 0.95 m clearance. **The cow does not move.**
t=47.0  Clear. Back to 38 km/h.
t=52.0  The junction. Three roads, no signal. A cyclist crosses. Dogs. Shop.
t=62.0  End.

## WHAT THIS TESTS — and why the baseline fails structurally
**An agent that does not react to you at all.** A purely defensive planner waits for the road
to clear; a cow never clears it. That is the frozen-robot problem, and it is a *structural*
failure, not a tuned one.
Plus: an oncoming vehicle on the wrong side, and a commit that must be aborted and retaken.
