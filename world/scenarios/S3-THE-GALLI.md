# S3 · THE GALLI — the squeeze, and a real shortcut
**Location: the real map, centre (−155, −476), radius 185 m.** Inherits [S0].

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY THERE
1076 m of road in seven pieces, **every one of them residential**, and **not a single junction
of three or more roads** — they are simple links, which is exactly what a lane network is.
- **a 416 m lane running 175°** (nearly due south) — this is the spine
- a 278 m lane at 154°, a 106 m at 17°, a 105 m at 313°, 65 m at 133°, 64 m at 343°, 42 m at 58°
**The 654 m lane that starts on one main road and ends on another passes through here.**
That was the whole reason this location was chosen: it is a *genuine* shortcut, so a car
taking it is behaving sensibly, not being forced into a stunt for the demo.

## THE ROAD
Tagged residential, **4.5 m** where it is open — but **it does not stay 4.5 m.**
Real widths along the run, and these are what the scenario turns on:
| chainage | clear width | why |
|---|---|---|
| 0–90 m | 4.5 m | normal lane |
| 90–150 m | 3.6 m | buildings step forward, no setback |
| 150–205 m | **3.2 m** | tagged living_street width |
| 205–232 m | **2.4 m** | a first-floor projection and a buttress |
| **232–246 m** | **1.95 m** | **the squeeze — a scooter parked against the left wall** |
| 246–300 m | 3.0 m | opens again |
| 300–416 m | 3.8 m | approach to the main road |
Surface: **no markings anywhere.** Broken concrete in patches, brick paving in others, bare
earth in the worst 40 m. Camber replaced by a cross-fall toward the drain on one side.
**An open drain the whole length, right side**, 380 mm wide × 350 mm deep, brick-lined,
uncovered except for a slab at each doorway. **It is 300 mm below the road edge**, so the
usable width is less than the width between the walls.
No kerb. The road meets the wall directly or meets the drain lip.
**Two speed humps**, home-made, not IRC — mounded concrete 2.2 m long, 120 mm high, unpainted.

## OVERHEAD — this is half the look
The lane is roofed by wires. **9 to 14 parallel runs** at different sags: three 11 kV above,
four 415 V below, and the rest cable TV and telephone, spliced, drooping, bundled at two poles.
**Service drops to every single house**, sagging visibly lower than everything else, landing at
a meter box at 4.4 m. Two illegal taps.
**Drying washing on 6 balconies**, strung across the lane in two places at 4.8 m.
Two **bamboo poles wedged across** between buildings, holding nothing in particular.
Buildings on both sides step out above the ground floor, so the sky is a narrow strip.

## THE ACTION — 44 seconds
t=0     Ego enters the lane at **14 km/h**. Walls close in. Wires overhead.
t=6.5   Width down to 3.6 m. A child runs across at 8.2 s. Speed 9 km/h.
t=12.0  **Motorcycle oncoming.** Clear width 3.2 m; two vehicles need 3.6 m. Ego stops at a
        doorway recess and lets it through. **Which of us gives way is a decision, not a rule.**
t=17.4  Moving again. The home-made hump at 178 m. 6 km/h.
t=22.6  **THE SQUEEZE.** The scooter juts 0.72 m out. Drain lip 0.35 m in on the right.
        **Free width measured: 1.95 m.**
        Ego is **1.70 m** wide, **1.90 m with mirrors**. Margin: 25 mm each side.
        **This is a MEASUREMENT, not a judgement.** A human uses their eyes because they have
        no tape measure. We have a laser.
t=23.9  **Fold the mirrors — 1.70 m, margin 125 mm each side.** MirrorsFolded is a planner
        action, not a fixed vehicle property, and it is the cheapest 200 mm available.
t=24.8  Check the **swept path of the whole body**, not the centreline — the corners sweep wider
        when steering. Confirms it fits.
t=25.5  Creep through at **2 km/h**, barrier running at 50–100 Hz underneath. The dog moves.
t=31.0  Clear. Widens to 3.0 m. Back to 11 km/h.
t=38.0  The kirana shop, two people, a cow in a doorway.
t=44.0  Ego rejoins the main road. **The shortcut worked.**
        BASELINE: never entered. No lane, no reference path, no gap in a traffic stream — it
        has nothing to plan against.

## WHAT THIS TESTS
**That the tight space is a measurement problem, not a judgement problem.** Free width against
the real footprint including mirrors, checked over the swept path, with a speed-dependent margin
(centimetres at 2 km/h, 1.5 m at 40 km/h — exactly what a human does).
And the honest half: **if it does not fit, stop and declare blocked. Do not pretend.**
